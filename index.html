Drag a token from the tray onto the court to place it. Drag a token off the court (or double-click it) to remove it. Select a line tool, then drag from a token to draw a cut/pass/screen/dribble — click the tool again for move mode. Click a line to select it, then drag its curve handle to bend it, or drag its endpoint to move it, detach it from a token, or attach it to a different token. Double-click a line to remove it. Name a play and click Save to store it, then pick it from the list to Load or Delete it later.
